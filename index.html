Call script
  1. Call the business in the black box. It always shows who to call next: follow-ups that are due first, then people you haven't called.
  2. Tap what happened. The status and the next call date are set for you. Tapped the wrong one? Hit Undo.
  3. Send the demo. "WhatsApp demo" opens a ready message with their site link. If they agree, fill in the package and price.
